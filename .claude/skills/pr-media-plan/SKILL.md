## Hard rule: no local mekomonim - Leah rejected this explicitly, don't re-suggest it

An early draft of the media list included local Ramla/Lod community papers (mekomonim) as an easy first step. **Leah rejected this outright, sharply**: "אני לא מוציאה אנרגיה על מקומון... בשביל מה אני צריכה מקומון שיקבור אותי." Her reasoning: mekomonim reach mostly older local residents via local Facebook groups, not a national or viral audience - same effort as pitching real TV/press, for a fraction of the reach. **Do not propose local mekomonim again** unless she raises it herself. She wants only high-impact channels: TV, radio, and national press.
