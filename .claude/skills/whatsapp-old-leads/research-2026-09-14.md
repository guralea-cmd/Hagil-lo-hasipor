# WhatsApp old-leads campaign - research (2026-09-14)

Scope: one marketing template + one 14-day reminder to Leah's old Pilates leads, sent through Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API on a NEW Israeli number, with replies going to Google Sheets. This is web research only. Nothing was bought, signed up for, or logged into.
Exchange-rate assumption used below: **$1 = ₪3.7**. This is an assumption, not a sourced rate. Check the day's rate.

**Recommendation: a 019 Mobile eSIM line at about ₪19.80/month (or the ₪10/month basic line, if it's still offered when she orders). Setup is free with eSIM, or ₪10 for a physical SIM.**
Why this one:
- It is a real mobile number, so both the SMS and voice OTP arrive reliably. The virtual-number options each carry a documented OTP failure risk: Extra Mobile has no SMS and flaky voice calls; Zadarma won't guarantee WhatsApp.
- It is the cheapest reliable monthly cost, from an Israeli company, with no document upload to a foreign provider.
- The number is not tied to her own number.

Practical notes:
- The SIM is only needed to receive the one-time OTP, and again only if Meta ever asks to re-verify. Keep the line paid so the number is never recycled to someone else.
- **If "no SIM at all" is a hard requirement, the fallback is Extra Mobile at ₪36/month** (voice OTP only).
- Known issue with any new Israeli number: if it is a recycled number whose previous owner still has WhatsApp on it, Meta will say it's already registered. The commonly reported fix is to activate it once in the WhatsApp app, delete that account, then add it to the Cloud API. Test this first.

## 2. Meta Cloud API costs for Israel

- **Pricing model:** per-message pricing since **2025-07-01**. Meta charges only for **delivered template messages**. All non-template (free-form) messages are free, and utility templates sent inside an open customer-service window are free. Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a 72h free window. There is no general free tier for marketing. https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing
- **Israel rates** (Meta rate card effective 2026-04-01, as shown in a table "valid May 2026"): **marketing $0.0353**, utility $0.0053, authentication $0.0053 per delivered message. https://whautomate.com/whatsapp-business-api-pricing
  - Plivo's Israel page shows marketing at $0.0388. That is a BSP price and probably includes markup. https://www.plivo.com/whatsapp/pricing/il/
  - Meta's list of rate changes for 2026-07-01 and 2026-10-01 does not include Israel.
  - Confirm the live rate in WhatsApp Manager before sending, since the official rate card is only viewable on Meta's interactive page: https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/platform-pricing/
- **Replies:** when a lead replies, a 24h customer-service window opens. Leah's free-form answers inside that window cost **$0**.
- **Billing currency:** ILS is **not** one of Meta's billing currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.), so she will be billed in USD.

**Estimated cost** at $0.0353, counting only delivered messages. Numbers not on WhatsApp are not charged:

| Recipients | 1st message only | 1st + reminder to everyone (upper bound) |
|---|---|---|
| 300 | $10.59 (~₪39) | $21.18 (~₪78) |
| 1,000 | $35.30 (~₪131) | $70.60 (~₪261) |

In practice it will be lower: the reminder goes only to people who didn't reply or ask for removal, and some numbers aren't on WhatsApp.

- **Payment method:** a valid payment method on the WhatsApp Business Account is required before paid template messages can be sent.
  - Where: Business Settings → Billing & payments → Payment methods → Add.
  - Who: someone with the Admin or Finance role.
  - What card: Meta accepts regular bank credit/debit cards, **not prepaid or virtual cards**. Choose the country and currency first.
  - Sources: https://help.chatarmin.com/en/meta-related/add-a-payment-method-to-your-whatsapp-business-account , https://support.bolddesk.com/kb/article/18142/how-to-add-a-payment-method-in-whatsapp-cloud-api-integration
- **Messaging limits** (https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messaging-limits):
  - Newly created portfolios start at **250 unique recipients per rolling 24h** (outside service windows).
  - The limit rises to 1,000 after **business verification**, or after sending 2,000 high-quality delivered messages in 30 days.
  - Since Oct 2025 the limit is **per business portfolio** and shared by all numbers in it. Her existing portfolio 141699730270272 may already have a different limit, so check WhatsApp Manager.
  - With 250/day, 1,000 leads take 4+ days. Spreading the sends out is actually better for quality.
- **Per-user marketing cap:** Meta may not deliver a marketing template to a user who has recently received many marketing messages from any business. The error is **131049**; wait at least 24h before retrying. It doesn't apply to the EEA/UK/Japan/Korea, so it does apply to Israel. https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/marketing-templates/per-user-limits

3. **L** - Set the business profile **display name**.
   - It must match how the brand appears on her website (capitalization and spacing), be at least 3 characters, and relate to the business.
   - The name is reviewed at registration, after business verification, and when the portfolio reaches the 2,000 limit.
   - Messages **can be sent before approval**; users may see the number instead of the name. https://docs.360dialog.com/docs/resources/phone-numbers/display-names
4. **L** - Add the phone number and receive the **OTP** (SMS or voice call, 6 digits) on the new line.

6. **L** - **Add the payment method** (card details; Admin/Finance role).
7. **L (optional but recommended)** - **Business verification.**
   - It raises the limit from 250 to 1,000/day and the number cap from 2 to 20.
   - It requires admin role and documents showing the legal name plus address or phone (business license/registration, utility bill).
   - Review usually takes about 2 business days and can take up to 30 days. https://docs.360dialog.com/docs/resources/meta-business-verification , https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/40116148219163
8. **L** - System User token:
   - Business Settings → System users → the existing System User → **Assign assets**: the app (Manage app / full control) and the WABA (full control).

   - Store it in the gitignored `secrets.json`.

## 4. Template rules and Leah's text

- **Category:** MARKETING is correct for re-engagement/promotion. Submitting it as Utility would get it recategorized or rejected. Categories: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/template-categorization
- **Variables** (Meta, "Template review" - https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/template-review):
  - The body may **not start or end with a parameter** ("dangling parameters are not allowed").
  - Parameters must be sequential with matching braces.
  - Don't use too many parameters relative to the length.
  - **"היי {{1}}," is OK**: it starts with the static word "היי", not with `{{1}}`.
  - A sample value for `{{1}}` must be supplied when submitting (e.g. "מיכל").
- **Other rejection reasons:**
  - content violating the Commerce/Business Policy
  - threatening or abusive content
  - duplicates of an existing template
  - variables with special characters (#, $, %) - https://docs.aws.amazon.com/social-messaging/latest/userguide/managing-templates_rejection.html
- **Review time:** up to 24h; appeals are also decided within 24h.
- **Hebrew:** supported as a template language (code `he`). I didn't open Meta's language list directly; BSP language-code lists include Hebrew.
- **Opt-out:** Meta *recommends* the **"Marketing opt-out" quick-reply button** (e.g. "Stop promotions" → Hebrew "הסרה"). It helps the quality rating and lets users stop marketing messages. It is recommended, not mandatory. https://support.qiscus.com/hc/en-us/articles/13766924007577 , https://www.facebook.com/business/help/448422200528701

**Leah's text - flags:**
1. **Won't cause Meta rejection:** the variable placement is fine, the text is well over the parameter ratio, and there are no links, prices, or special characters in the variable.
2. **Israeli-law gap (not a Meta issue):** section 30A(e) requires ad messages to start with the word **"פרסומת"** and to include the sender's name plus contact/address and the right to refuse and how.
   - Present: her name, the studio, and the "הסרה" instruction.
   - Missing: "פרסומת" at the start, and an address or contact detail.
   - Adding "פרסומת:" before "היי {{1}}," keeps the template valid, because it still starts with text.
   - Wording changes are for Leah to decide.
3. The **health/body question** ("איך את מרגישה היום עם הגוף?") is low risk. Fitness services aren't a restricted category, and I found no rule against it.
4. **Feminine grammar throughout** (את / תשלחי / כתבי) doesn't affect approval, but is wrong for any male leads. Consider a separate list or a neutral version.
5. "הסרה" typed in the body works for the automation. If the opt-out button is added, the webhook gets a `button` reply as well, and both must be handled.

**14-day reminder:** a second template is **not required**, because an approved template can be sent again. A separate reminder template is better, though: resending the same text reads as spam and hurts quality. It needs its own approval (~24h) and must also follow the variable and "פרסומת" rules. Send it only to people who didn't reply and didn't ask for removal.

## 5. Compliance risks (summary, not legal advice)

**WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy** (https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/ , https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in):
- A business may contact people only if (a) they gave the business their phone number **and** (b) the business received **opt-in** permission to message them.
- The opt-in must state the business name and that they'll receive messages. It can be general, not WhatsApp-specific, "as long as businesses comply with all local laws".
- Opt-out requests must be honored on or off WhatsApp.
- Enforcement: users block or report the business, and low quality over time leads to rate limits and restrictions.
- **Risk:** very old leads who only inquired probably never opted in to messages, so block/report rates may be high. That lowers the quality rating, which can cut the messaging limit or flag the number. Mitigations: send in small daily batches, start with the most recent leads, keep the opt-out easy, and stop if quality drops to yellow or red.

**Israel - Communications Law section 30A ("חוק הספאם"):**
- **Prohibition:** sending a "דבר פרסומת" by fax, auto-dialer, electronic message, or SMS without **explicit prior consent** (opt-in). https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/חוק_הספאם
- **WhatsApp coverage:** Hebrew Wikipedia notes instant messaging isn't named explicitly. Several Israeli law-firm guides say courts treat WhatsApp messages as covered (e.g. https://www.smallclaims.co.il/תביעה-בגין-הודעות-ספאם/ , https://moticohenadv.com/spam-law-email-marketing/). **Treat it as covered.**
- **Exception - existing customer / negotiation (30A(c)):** all conditions are required:
  - the recipient gave their details during a purchase **or negotiation** for a product or service;
  - the advertiser **told them the details would be used for advertising**;
  - the advertiser gave them a chance to refuse, and they didn't;
  - the ad is for a similar product or service.
  - An inquiry about Pilates classes may count as negotiation, but old leads only qualify if **the notice and the chance to refuse were given at the time** (e.g. text on the form they filled in). Sources: moticohenadv.com, aviveilon.law FAQ (https://aviveilon.law/מאמרים/faq-דואר-זבל/).
- **Exception - one-time consent request:** this applies only to a recipient that is a **business**, not to private individuals, so it doesn't cover her leads. Sources: aviveilon.law, mach-law opinion.
- **Required in every ad (30A(e)):** the word **"פרסומת"** at the start, the sender's name and address/contact, and the **right to refuse and how**. Refusal must be possible free of charge, through the same channel (moticohenadv.com).
- **Damages:** courts may award **up to ₪1,000 per message without proof of damage**. Class actions are possible. One source also cites a criminal fine of up to ₪202,000 (smallclaims.co.il).
- **What small businesses typically do:**
  - message only people who actually inquired;
  - put "פרסומת" at the start with name and contact;
  - offer one-word removal and honor it immediately;
  - keep a log of the source and date of each lead and each removal;
  - avoid repeated sends;
  - drop the oldest leads, where proof of the inquiry or consent is weakest.
  - Some ask a lawyer to review the text before a first campaign.

**Top 3 risks:**
1. Spam-law claims, up to ₪1,000 per message, from old leads who can't be shown to have consented or received the 30A(c) notice. A reminder doubles the exposure.
2. WhatsApp quality rating and number restriction if many cold recipients block or report.
3. Template or legal mismatch: the current text lacks "פרסומת" and contact details (legal gap), and the reminder needs its own approved template.

## 6. Automation without a paid platform - Apps Script as the webhook

- **Meta requirements:**
  - GET verification: check `hub.mode=subscribe` and `hub.verify_token`, then return the raw `hub.challenge` as plain text with status 200. JSON-wrapped responses fail.
  - POST events: Meta expects **HTTP 200**. Any other status triggers **retries for up to 7 days**, and duplicate notifications are normal ("at-least-once").
  - https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/webhooks/overview , https://hookdeck.com/webhooks/platforms/guide-to-whatsapp-webhooks-features-and-best-practices
- **Apps Script behavior:** a web app returns a **302 redirect** to `script.googleusercontent.com`, and the caller must follow it to get the response body. The script code (`doPost`) **still executes fully** even when the caller doesn't follow the redirect (execution logs show "completed"). https://groups.google.com/g/google-apps-script-community/c/WALz_3rw7u4 (Feb 2025)
- **Verification (GET):** tutorials use an Apps Script URL directly as the Meta callback with `ContentService.createTextOutput(challenge)` (purshology, 2026-03-02: https://www.purshology.com/2026/03/how-to-connect-whatsapp-api-with-google-sheets-free-method/ ; older Messenger bot: https://github.com/JZL/Facebook-bot-Google-Apps-Script). **No source explicitly confirms that Meta follows the 302.** Treat it as "reported to work, not documented". It's a 5-minute test: if "Verify and save" succeeds, it works.
- **Incoming events (POST):**
  - The Sheet write and the notification happen regardless, because the code runs.
  - Whether Meta counts the 302 as a failure (causing retries or duplicates) is undocumented.
  - Mitigation: **deduplicate on the message id (`wamid`)** before writing a row (with LockService). A duplicate "הסרה" or hot-lead row is then harmless.
- **Limitations:**
  - Apps Script `doPost` cannot read request headers, so it **can't verify `X-Hub-Signature-256`**. Mitigation: put a secret query parameter in the callback URL.
  - A notification to Leah by email (MailApp) is free. A WhatsApp notification from the business number outside a 24h window would need a paid template.
- **Verdict:** feasible for this small volume and free, provided (a) the GET verification test passes and (b) `wamid` deduplication is added.
- **If verification fails or retries pile up:** the cheapest reliable fix is a tiny free relay in front of Apps Script. A Cloudflare Worker (free plan) or Google Cloud Run function returns 200 to Meta immediately and forwards the JSON to the Apps Script URL. Cloud Run needs a Google Cloud billing account even within its free tier.
